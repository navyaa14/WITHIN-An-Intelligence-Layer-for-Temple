Method and limits

Evaluation

Every split is grouped by participant: nobody appears in both training and test. Headline numbers are balanced accuracy and macro-F1, because labels are imbalanced; raw accuracy and chance (always guessing the most common class) are shown alongside.

Every REFED participant watched the same 15 videos, so a second test holds out people and videos together. If the model had learned the videos rather than the response, this number drops.

Each person's signals are scaled using their own unlabelled recordings. In a product, that means a short calibration period before readings appear.

Replays use out-of-fold predictions for a held-out participant chosen before looking at the outcome. The reveal shows whatever happened.

Limits

fNIRS is not Flow: different sensor, placement and signal. Nothing here shows WITHIN would work on Temple hardware; it shows the question is worth asking.

Physiological states do not uniquely determine emotions. Arousal and valence are dimensions, not diagnoses.
